# Showroom QR Labels — A4 Print Layout v25

- Selected showroom articles generate a print-ready A4 PDF.
- Each label is exactly 100mm × 50mm (10cm × 5cm).
- A4 sheet contains 2 columns × 5 rows = 10 labels.
- If more than 10 articles are selected, additional A4 pages are generated automatically.
- Each label is product-description focused and does not use a product image.
- Each label includes: G logo, QR code, article/SKU, model when available, large product description, L × B × H, EAN, and prominent MRP.
- Removed from the label: icon set, “Scan to view product details” text, and “G-RECORDS | Article Ledger” text block.
- QR payload remains the same guest showroom URL format used by the working v24 scanner flow.
- The web preview is updated to visually match the print label.
