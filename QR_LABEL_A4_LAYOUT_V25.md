# Showroom QR Label A4 Layout

- A4 portrait PDF
- 10 labels per sheet (2 columns x 5 rows)
- Each physical label: exactly 100 mm x 50 mm
- No product image
- No SKU badge
- No G logo on the physical label
- No scan-instruction text on the physical label
- Product description is the primary visual element
- Large MRP block
- Model (when available), L x B x H and EAN
- QR opens the guest showroom product-details URL
- More than 10 selected products continue onto additional A4 pages
- Light cut guides are printed around the label grid
